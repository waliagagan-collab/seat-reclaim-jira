## 1. What I found

Severity: **high** = a buyer or a reviewer is misled or loses something; **medium** = worth fixing before the next
listing edit or release; **low** = small.

### The app

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |

| A1 | medium | Two messages told the admin to reinstall the app when Jira refuses a read. A reinstall is a new installation with empty storage (Forge docs, hosted storage data lifecycle: the old data can only be linked again by request within 21 days; the privacy page also says uninstalling deletes the app's storage), so History and every Undo would be gone. | Fixed |
| A2 | low | Two scan warnings used API words ("Application roles were not readable", "No application roles with licensing groups") and did not say that nobody on such a list can be reclaimed. | Fixed |
| A3 | low | Progress and warning text used long dashes and the word "candidates". | Fixed |
| A4 | low | The Rovo agent called a History entry "Failed" where the page says "Not done", and passed the raw word "pending" where the page says "Interrupted". | Fixed |
| A5 | low | Two spellings on screen: "license" and "licence". | Fixed |
| A6 | medium | The app stores Atlassian account IDs (list, History, protected list) and does not report them to Atlassian's personal data reporting API. Left by the 10 Nov audit for the owner; still open. History is kept for good, so the ID of a closed account stays in it. Atlassian's user privacy guide for Forge says "All apps storing personal data must report user personal data"; it does not say whether an ID by itself counts. | Not changed: needs the `report:personal-data` scope, a major version |
| A7 | low | Left by the 10 Nov audit, still open: the per-shard user map of a running scan passes the storage value limit at about 20,000 to 30,000 licensed users; there is no per-user seat check after a reclaim (the page shows Atlassian's product totals before and after). | Not changed |

remote, and the deploy output confirms the build is eligible for Runs on Atlassian; the only log line

Two of the 10 Nov "needs a live check" items are now settled from what is on record: the scan's group read works

(audit 14 Nov 2026)". In short:

- **Name:** no change.

## 6. What I chose not to change, and why

- **Personal data reporting (A6).** It needs a new scope, so a major version. Proposal for the owner: add `report:personal-data`, report the stored account IDs on Atlassian's cycle (7 days by default) from a scheduled trigger, and for a closed account remove it from the list and the protected list and blank its ID in History.
- **"Jira Service Desk" in the product column.** That is the name Jira returns for the product. The name is also the key for saved seat prices and for stored History and list rows, so renaming it on screen needs a mapping in the page, both exports and the agent. Not small, and it would change `srj-1` and `srj-3`.
- **"The app takes them out of the groups that give them that product"** in How it works reads a little oddly ("that product"). Left as it is so `srj-3-how.png` stays word for word right.
- **Plan limit shown as "35000"** without a separator. Same reason: it is in both usable screenshots.
- **The logo and banner files in `marketplace/assets/`.** They are the earlier design. I did not replace them: the banners cannot be redrawn here and `build_assets.py` draws the old design. `listing.md` now says not to upload them. Either delete them or rebuild them from `../app-icons`.
- **The review request** ("Did this save you money?"). It offers nothing in return, shows only after a successful reclaim, and is remembered per site once closed. Left.
- **Price.** The market study suggests about $300 a year at 50 users (now $450). Owner's decision; no text depends on it.
- **A line about moving off Connect apps** (market study, move 2). Not added to More details: there is no room under 1000 characters without dropping something a buyer needs, and I could not check the date myself.
- **The large-site limit of the scan's user map and the per-user seat check (A7).** Both are separate jobs, as the 10 Nov audit said.
- **The website** (L5). Outside this folder. The sentences to change are quoted in L5; the 10 Nov fix report has the full old and new text.
